## Notes for this board
- GPIO45 must be low at reset (or VDD_SPI fixed by eFuse). A pull-up on GPIO45 selects 1.8 V and the in-package 3.3 V flash/PSRAM will not boot.
- 7 GPIOs are taken by the in-package flash/PSRAM (SPICS0/SPICS1, CLK, D, Q, WP, HD; pins 28-35) and are not free (p4, p28).
- The modem-sleep table is stated for S3/S3FH8; PSRAM parts "may be slightly higher" (p62).
- RF figures are peak values at 100 % TX duty; average current for the sim depends on the duty cycle.
- The 3.3 V rail must stay ≥3.0 V at the ~340 mA TX peak. With the ME6217's 100-180 mV dropout at 300 mA, VSW ≥ ~3.2 V keeps the rail above 3.0 V, and VSW ≥ ~3.5 V keeps it in regulation.
- Main crystal is required (p38). Its frequency and load caps are not specified in this edition.
