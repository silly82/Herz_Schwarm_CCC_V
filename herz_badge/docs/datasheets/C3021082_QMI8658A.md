## Notes for this board
- Pinout matches the expected list, with one difference: pins 10/11 are not plain NC. Pin 10 (RESV) must NOT go to GND; tie it to VDDIO or leave it open (internal pull-up). Pin 11 (RESV-NC) is an output by default and should float. The p11 figure still labels them CS-AUX / SDO-AUX.
- CS (pin 12) high selects I2C. It has a 200 k pull-up; tie it to VDDIO for a stable level. SDx/SCx: VDDIO, GND or open.
- SA0 is inverted from the usual convention: tied low = 0x6B, high/open = 0x6A.
- Running at 3.3 V is within range; datasheet currents are at 1.8 V, so expect slightly more at 3.3 V (not specified).
- The rail must not glitch during the 15 ms init (p16). Watch LED-current steps on the shared 3.3 V LDO.
