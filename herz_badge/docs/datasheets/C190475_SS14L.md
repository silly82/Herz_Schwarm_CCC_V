## Notes for this board

- D101, VBUS to VSYS: carries the whole load on USB. At 1 A it dissipates about 0.55 W, a 60 °C rise; a white flash
  at brightness 130 (about 1.4 A for 150 ms) is within its surge rating, but the firmware power limit (DESIGN.md D17)
  keeps the average below 1 A.
- On battery it is reverse-biased by VSYS minus VBUS, a few volts: leakage there is far below the 0.5 mA at 40 V,
  and R105 (10 kΩ) holds VBUS down.
