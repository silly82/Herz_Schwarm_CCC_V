## Notes for this board
- VBUS -> VSYS on USB: VSYS ≈ 5.0 - 0.30 = 4.70 V at 0.1 A and ≈ 4.60 V at 0.5 A (25 °C typical).
- **Limit:** P_D = 250 mW with 400 °C/W caps continuous DC at about 0.55 A (0.25 W / ~0.45 V) at 25 °C, and less when warm. The 1 A I_O rating is not usable continuously. On USB the LED matrix plus LDO all flow through this diode, so firmware must keep VSYS current near 0.5 A on USB, or the diode needs upsizing.
- On battery the diode is reverse-biased by ≈VSYS. Its leakage (≈5 µA at 25 °C, tens of µA when warm) flows into the VBUS node and through the 100 k gate pull-down to GND. That is a continuous battery drain. It lifts VBUS to ≈0.5 V at 25 °C, which is harmless for the P-FET gate and TP4054 UVLO.
- Hot-case check (from the 75 °C curve, ≈30 µA even at <1 V reverse): 100 k would let the VBUS node float up to ≈3 V. That brings the load-sharing FET's V_GS toward its threshold, and the cell would then feed VSYS through the body diode. A stiffer gate pull-down (e.g. 10-47 k) avoids this.
