# Herz-Schwarm Badge PCB layout

Status (2026-10-17): `boards/badge/` placed, not routed: 30 × 34 mm, 4 layers, DRC 0 errors
(the unrouted nets aside), 0 schematic-parity issues. `boards/reference/` is schematic only.

## Floor plan

The reference, from Waveshare's dimension drawing and product photos (top view, front = LED side):

- Outline 25.00 × 25.00 mm, corner radius 1.0 mm.
- Header pads in two columns 22.86 mm apart, 1.07 mm in from the left and right edges, 10 pads each at 2.54 mm
  pitch, the first 1.07 mm below the top edge.
- USB-C on the back at the top edge, overhanging it and off-centre; the drawing (front view) gives 7.70 mm from the
  connector's right end to the right pad column.
- Front: the 8 × 8 LEDs only, pitch about 2.6–2.8 mm (estimated from the photo, to be measured).
- Back (as seen from the back): ESP32-S3 in the middle, IMU to its right, RESET left and BOOT right of the USB-C,
  crystal below the chip, chip antenna at the bottom edge in the middle, the DOUT/5V/GND pads beside the IO36/IO37
  header pads.

For the clone, the back must also take the charger, two P-FETs, a Schottky, the slide switch and the cell connector.
Dropping the header (D7) frees a 2.2 mm strip along each side. The antenna needs its keep-out at an edge whichever
way D6 goes. Try placements with a mock-up before the schematic (pcb-kit's placement mock-up and placement-study skill).

### Badge (`boards/badge/`, generated by `scripts/place_badge.py`)

Board coordinates from the top-left corner, top view (front = LEDs), millimetres.

- Outline 30 × 34, corner radius 1.0 (D11: a 103030 cell sits flush behind it). Aux origin (fab files) at the
  bottom-left corner.
- Front: 8 × 8 WS2812B-2020 at 3.2 pitch, centres x 3.8–26.2, y 2.6–25.0, chain index 0 top-left, rows run left to
  right (DESIGN.md 2.1: confirm on a real badge before routing). Below them: the charge LED (D102, R104) and
  "Herz-Schwarm".
- Bottom strip (y > 26.3), back: antenna AE201 (Walsin RFANT3216120A5T) in the bottom-left corner as its datasheet
  draws it: centre (2.34, 32.26), long axis along the bottom edge 0.91 mm in, fed from the right through L203
  (6.8 nH) at (5.30, 32.26). Rule area x 0–6, y 28–34 on all copper layers: no pours, no vias, no parts other than
  those two; ground with a via fence along its two inner sides. USB-C J101 at x 14.7 with its mouth 1.15 mm over the
  edge, slide switch SW101 at x 25.2 with its lever about 0.8 mm over the edge. These are the parts with drilled pegs or a keep-out, which is why
  the strip exists (D8).
- Back, behind the matrix: ESP32-S3 U201 at (10, 19), LNA_IN corner toward the antenna; JST PH J102 at the right
  edge, mouth outward; RESET top right and BOOT top left as seen from the back; IMU, LDO and level shifter in the
  upper half; charger and the two P-FETs below the JST.
- `place_badge.py` places the big and edge parts from its FIXED table and packs every other part at the free spot
  nearest the pin it serves (PACKED), using the courtyards; it reports overlaps and drilled pads under parts on the
  other side. It rebuilds the board on each run, so after routing starts, positions are edited in KiCad instead.
- Fits behind it: a 25 × 30 mm LiPo pouch.

For routing (not started): USB D± leave the ESP32 on the side away from the connector (pins 25/26 face up), so they
run around the chip, which full-speed USB tolerates; the crystal sits at the RF corner, so keep ground between its
traces and the RF feed; the RF feed is a 50 Ω coplanar line from pin 1 through R207 to L203 and AE201, entering the
keep-out at y 32.26; the TP4056's exposed pad needs its thermal vias into the ground planes; D101 carries up to
about 1.4 A on USB, so its traces and VSYS want width; the LEDs need VSW and GND planes (In1/In2) with vias at each
LED. Walsin measured the antenna on a 1.2 mm board: order 1.2 mm to stay close to it.

## Stackup and rules

4 layers. JLC's 4-layer limits are in `boards/badge/badge.kicad_pro` (0.1 mm track and clearance, 0.45/0.2 mm vias,
0.3 mm to the edge). Stackup and impedance for the RF line are set when routing.
