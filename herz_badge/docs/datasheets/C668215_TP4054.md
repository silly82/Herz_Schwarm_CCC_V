## Notes for this board
- R_PROG = 3.3 k gives 303 mA nominal; only the 10 k and 2 k points are tested (±7 %), so expect roughly 280-325 mA.
- Termination ≈ 30 mA and trickle ≈ 30 mA at 3.3 k (C/10 scaling); recharge starts near 4.1 V.
- Power: (5 V - V_BAT) x 0.303 A is 0.24-0.6 W, above the 250 mW P_D rating whenever V_BAT is below ~4.17 V. Thermal regulation (≈120 °C) will reduce the current for most of the CC phase. θJA is not given, and the "Thermal Considerations" section the text refers to is missing.
- Battery drain from the charger with USB removed: 6 µA typ, 10 µA max. The text on p3 says "<2 µA" when VCC - V_BAT < 30 mV, which conflicts with the table. Use the table.
- VCC pin needs ≥1 µF bypass (p3); the application circuits use 4.7 µF on VCC and 10 µF on BAT (p9).
- Abs-max "PROG VSS-0.3~VSS+0.3 V" (p4) is clearly a typo; PROG sits at 1 V and can float to ~2.4 V.
