## Notes for this board
- The project BOM assumes 40 MHz with a 10 pF load. The datasheet confirms 10 pF is an available option, but not that this PN is 10 pF / ±10 ppm. Check the LCSC listing or ask JGHC.
- With 10 pF per side the crystal sees C_L = 10 x 10 / 20 = 5 pF plus stray (typically 2-5 pF), about 7-10 pF. For a 10 pF crystal that is at or below target, so the oscillator runs slightly fast. The usual sizing C1 = C2 = 2 x (C_L - C_stray) gives ~12-16 pF for C_L = 10 pF (calculation, not from the datasheet). Measure the frequency offset on a prototype; 12 pF caps may fit better.
- Pins 2/4 to GND matches KiCad's Crystal_GND24 symbol.
