## Notes for this board
- Pinout confirmed (1 DOUT, 2 GND, 3 DIN, 4 VDD). The custom footprint (pads at ±0.915, ±0.55 mm, 0.7 mm square) is a 180° rotation of the datasheet top view, which is consistent.
- **Minimum VDD is 3.3 V.** On battery, VSW ≈ V_BAT - 2 x I x R_DS(on), so it falls below 3.3 V once the cell is under ~3.4-3.5 V (sooner under LED load). No minimum VDD for full colour is given; blue and green headroom is not specified.
- Full white = 36 mA x 64 = 2.3 A. That exceeds the JST PH 2 A rating and the B5819WS's practical ~0.5 A. Firmware must cap brightness and total current.
- Static current ≤1 µA x 64 = ≤64 µA on VSW when all LEDs are off.
- VIH = 0.55 x VSW ≈ 2.5-2.6 V on USB. The level shifter output (≈VSW) meets it with margin.
- No luminous-efficiency vs current or thermal derating data is given.
