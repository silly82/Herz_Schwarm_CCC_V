## Notes for this board
- Pin-compatible with the ST part (same 1-6 mapping), so the KiCad Power_Protection:USBLC6-2SC6 symbol fits.
- V_RWM is only 5.0 V. USB VBUS may reach 5.25 V (5.5 V on some chargers), above the rated working voltage, though still below V_BR min of 6 V. The ST part is specified to 5.25 V.
- Leakage of 0.08 µA from VBUS only matters with USB connected. It is no battery drain, since VBUS is unpowered on battery.
- Only I/O-I/O capacitance is given, which is enough for USB full speed on the ESP32-S3's native USB.
- Clamp at 11-12 V is fine for VBUS (TP4054 VCC abs max 7 V, transient "11 V withstand"). Place it at the connector.
