## Notes for this board
- 50 mA rating: fine for switching the P-FET gate (≈40 µA through the 100 k). It must never carry load current.
- The text says "1 pole, 1 throw" (p4) but the part has three terminals. The drawing gives ②-③ in one position and does not state which position closes ②-①. Wire ② (common) and one outer pin, then confirm ON/OFF direction against the knob on a sample.
- Unequal 1.5 / 3.0 mm pitch: check the footprint (KiCad SW_SPDT_Shouhan_MSK12C02) against p1.
- Only one reflow pass is allowed. On a double-sided build, place the switch on the second side.
- In the ON position the 100 k gate pull-up draws VSYS / 100 k ≈ 30-47 µA continuously (a circuit property, not from this datasheet).
