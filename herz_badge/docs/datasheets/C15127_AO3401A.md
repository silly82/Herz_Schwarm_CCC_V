## Notes for this board
- Load-sharing FET (D = VBAT, S = VSYS): the body diode conducts VBAT -> VSYS, as intended. On battery, V_GS = -V_BAT (-3.0 to -4.2 V), so R_DS(on) is about 50-85 mΩ. With USB, the gate sits at VBUS (5 V) and the source at ≈4.6 V, so the FET is off.
- High-side switch: V_GS = -VSYS (-3.0 V on a flat cell up to ≈-4.6 V on USB), which is well past V_th max of -1.3 V. Specified R_DS(on) points stop at -2.5 V.
- Both FETs in series give ≈0.1-0.17 Ω from cell to VSW: ≈0.2-0.35 V drop at 2 A of LED current.
- V_GS stays within ±12 V everywhere in this circuit. The 100 k pull-up across 645 pF C_iss gives a turn-off τ of ≈65 µs.
- Nothing here contradicts the circuit.
