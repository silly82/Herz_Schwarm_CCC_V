## Notes for this board

- R_PROG = 3.0 kΩ gives 400 mA (1200 V / 3.0 kΩ); the termination is then about 40 mA.
- TEMP to GND disables the NTC input (p3); CE to VCC; STDBY left open (one red LED on CHRG).
- At 400 mA from 5 V into a 3.0–4.2 V cell the chip dissipates 0.3–0.8 W: within its 1.5 W, with the exposed pad
  soldered to the ground plane through vias (KiCad footprint SOIC-8-1EP_3.9x4.9mm_P1.27mm_EP2.41x3.3mm_ThermalVias).
- Replaces the TP4054 (C668215_TP4054.md), whose 250 mW absolute maximum a 300 mA charge exceeds (DESIGN.md D14).
